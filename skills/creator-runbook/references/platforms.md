# Masters for each destination

Recheck the destination's own help page on the day you deliver. Caps move. The rows below are the working defaults as checked on 26 November 2026, with the page that was actually opened. A row marked "recheck" was not confirmed on that platform's own page in this pass.

Export one finished master per aspect the commission needs. A second aspect is a reframe: new crop, new type size, new first second when the player is vertical. It is not a 16:9 file with bars, and it is not the long cut pasted into a square.

When the brief is "everywhere", ship two films and the covers:

- A long master, 16:9, for the places where someone watches a documentary or an essay.
- A short master, 9:16, whose claim is on screen before the first second ends.
- Covers: a 16:9 frame, a 9:16 frame, and a 3:4 frame when Xiaohongshu is in the list.

Same consistency document. Same grade. The short is a new edit. Write each post from the cut that will actually upload there. Translation is its own pass. See [posting](posting.md) for the words.

Container for every master: MP4, H.264, yuv420p, AAC-LC, 48 kHz, stereo, moov atom at the front. Keep the frame rate constant. Do not interlace.

## Type on a vertical master

Design the words inside the middle of the 1080×1920 frame. Usernames, captions, and buttons cover the top band and a deep bottom band, and the right edge on several of these players. A shared safe habit: keep titles inside roughly the center 80 percent of the width, clear of the top 14 percent and the bottom 22 percent. Tighten that for the one player you are cutting, after you look at it. Do not put the claim on the lower third of a Reel.

On a 16:9 long master, keep titles out of the lower band where the player chrome and closed captions sit. A source line can live there if it still reads when captions are on. Prefer the caption file for the full sentence.

## What not to ship

- One render "for social", letterboxed into every slot.
- The long film's cold open uploaded as a Short without a new first second.
- Burned-in English as the Bilibili, Douyin, or Xiaohongshu master. Give those cuts their own type.
- A caption that repeats a title already burned in, unless the sound needs it.
