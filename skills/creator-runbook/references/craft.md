# Picture, type, and joins

The finished frame should sit next to a current broadcaster or a serious channel. A default card, a glowing dot, a stock whoosh, or a transition preset is not a film.

The creator does not open an editor. If a shot has no asset, research it and produce the picture, the sound, and the type. Say what you made. A generated scene that could be mistaken for a record is labeled as a reconstruction or an illustration.

## Consistency

Write one `consistency.md` before the second shot when any of these will recur, or when more than one cut will ship: a person, an object, a place, a palette, a type style, a grade, a motif, a voice. One document for the whole film. Platform cuts point at it. They do not restate it.

Keep these headings:

- Logline. One sentence for what the last shot leaves with the viewer.
- Claims. Each spoken or burned line that states a fact, with the source locator. A reconstruction is marked.
- Palette. A few hex roles: ground, light, type, quiet type. One accent.
- Type. Families, the roles they play, the size steps, the tracking, the case. The scripts the faces actually cover.
- Frame. The masters, the grid, the safe area for type on each master.
- Light and grade. The direction of the key, what stays warm or cool, the one curve for the film.
- Motif. The thing that returns, and the new job it has the second time.
- Voice. One speaker and one engine for the film. The pace of each line is in [voice](voice.md).
- Joins. Which transitions this film uses, and what each one means.
- Canon. File paths for the references later shots must match.
- Do not drift. The list of traits that stay put: screen direction, wardrobe, eyeline, the wording of a repeated fact.

## Typography

Set every word in a font engine or in HTML and CSS. An image model does not paint titles, numbers, captions, or diagrams that have to be correct.

- One family for titles and one for information, or one family in two optical sizes. A new face per card is drift.
- At most four roles: kicker, title, line, source. One of them is loud.
- Kickers are short, tracked, and quiet. Titles are set, not stretched. Lines are short enough to read aloud once.
- Hang punctuation. Align to the grid. Leave the platform's buttons and captions a clear margin, measured on that master.
- A word appears when the picture has earned it. No bounce, no typewriter preset, no word that slides in for its own sake.
- Check contrast on the actual frame, at the size the viewer will use. Phone type is larger and shorter than a television lower third.
- If the language needs Chinese, Arabic, Devanagari, or another script, use a face that contains those glyphs. Do not fake them with a Latin fallback.
- Burned-in titles and the caption file do different jobs. Captions still carry the words and the meaningful sounds.

## Joins

A cut is the default. Name the join, and use a softer one only when it means something.

| Join | Use it when | Typical length at 24 fps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cut | A new fact, a match of action, direction, or eyeline | 0 |
| Dissolve | Time passes, or one thought continues into the next | 8–16 frames |
| Fade through black | A chapter ends | 12–24 frames |
| Match on shape or light | The outgoing frame and the incoming frame share an edge, a direction, or a highlight | 0, or a short dissolve if the light itself continues |

A whip, a zoom blur, a glitch, or a light leak is a single event with a reason, or it is not in the film. J-cut and L-cut the sound when the next place should arrive before or after the picture. Do not duck the whole track under a whoosh.

Author the join in the picture. The assembler may concatenate finished shots. Hard cuts between undesigned cards are not a style.

## Picture

Show the thing the sentence is about. A shot has thickness, a camera, and a light: a rendered scene, a photograph, or supplied footage. A measurement is a solid in that scene, with the real quantity and the axis held still. Stage the subject in the light. Do not paste a cut-out onto a backdrop. How the frame is rendered is in [the render path](render.md).

Hold a shot long enough to read the fact it introduces. Then leave. Coverage is a new angle that answers a new question, not a second version of the same frame.

## Grade and sound

One curve for the film. Skin and practical lights stay plausible. Titles use the same ivory, black, or accent as the picture. They do not arrive in a second graphic package.

Build the track from the picture: room, mechanism, breath, a motif that returns. Music marks a decision or a passage of time. Silence is a shot. Write down what the silence is for. Original or cleared sound only.

## Before you call it done

Watch the opening, the turn, and the last shot at phone size with sound, then with the picture muted, then with the sound alone. Read every number on screen against the source. If a join does not change the meaning, make it a cut.
