# Cordouan, 25 July 1823

## Logline

A lighthouse lens can be large without being thick, and that is what let Cordouan’s light reach.

## Claims

- On 25 July 1823 the first Fresnel lens installed in a lighthouse was lit at Cordouan, in the Gironde estuary. [Phare de Cordouan](https://www.phare-de-cordouan.fr/fresnel-et-sa-lentille-a-lhonneur-en-2023/), [Centre des monuments nationaux](https://cordouan.culture.gouv.fr/en/cordouans-first-lens).
- The lens keeps the curvature that bends the light and removes the thickness of a solid block of glass. That is the mechanism the pictures demonstrate. The 1823 optic also used mirrors and a lamp of several wicks. The film does not say the flame was left unchanged, and it does not draw those mirrors.
- The schematic is a principle, not a measured section of the 1823 apparatus.
- Every photograph in the cut is a generated reconstruction. The tower in the water is not a measured likeness of Cordouan. The word “Cordouan” appears only on the end card, as the name of the place where the lens was lit.
